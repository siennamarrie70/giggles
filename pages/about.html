Short animated comedy sketches about everyday adult life, new every day.

About this site

This site belongs to the small piece of software that runs the channel for its owner. Every day it:

  • looks at upcoming holidays, world days and what’s trending around the world, to pick a situation people recognise;
  • designs a small cast of grown-up cartoon characters and writes a short sketch for them, with the help of Google’s Gemini;
  • gives every character a voice with open-source text-to-speech, moves their mouths with the words and animates the sketch, all on the owner’s computer, with music by Kevin MacLeod (incompetech.com), credited in every description as its licence asks;
  • uploads the Short to the channel as private and schedules it to go public, so the owner can review it first;
  • reads the channel’s view counts to learn which sketches people enjoy.

Sign in with Google

The software uses Google sign-in for one account only: the channel owner’s. With that permission it uploads and schedules videos on the owner’s own channel and reads that channel’s statistics. It isn’t offered to the public and never accesses anyone else’s account.
