60-second case studies told by two cartoon hosts: why companies rise and fall, what economic decisions really do, and the stories behind history and myths. New every day.

About this site

This site belongs to the small piece of software that runs the channel for its owner. Every day it:

  • picks a question worth answering (about business, the economy, the week’s news, history or a myth) from what’s trending, Wikipedia’s news and history pages, and viewers’ suggestions;
  • researches it in free public sources (Wikipedia, news sites, IMF and World Bank data) and checks every fact against them, with the help of Google’s Gemini;
  • writes the hosts’ script, has every line checked against its sources again, and lists those sources in each video’s description;
  • animates it on the owner’s computer: open-source voices and lip-sync, charts and maps drawn from the data, and photos with free licences from Wikimedia Commons, with music by Kevin MacLeod (incompetech.com); photos and music are credited in every description as their licences ask;
  • uploads the Short to the channel as private and schedules it to go public, so the owner can review it first (cases about the news or politics wait for the owner’s approval);
  • reads the channel’s view counts and comments to learn which cases people enjoy and what they’d like next.

Sign in with Google

The software uses Google sign-in for one account only: the channel owner’s. With that permission it uploads and schedules videos on the owner’s own channel, reads that channel’s statistics and the public comments on its own videos. It isn’t offered to the public and never accesses anyone else’s account.
