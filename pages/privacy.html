Privacy policy

Your data, plainly

$channel · last updated $updated

This policy explains what the software behind the $channel YouTube channel (“the app”) does with Google user data. The app is a personal tool, used only by the channel’s owner.

YouTube API Services

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. How Google handles data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

What the app accesses

Only with the channel owner’s permission, given through Google sign-in:

  • Upload videos (youtube.upload): to upload Shorts to the owner’s channel and set their title, description, tags and publish time.
  • View the YouTube account (youtube.readonly): to read the channel’s name, its videos’ view, like and comment counts, and the public list of popular Comedy videos.
  • View YouTube Analytics reports (yt-analytics.readonly): to read aggregate statistics for the channel’s own videos, such as the average percentage watched and subscribers gained.

The app doesn’t access email, contacts, files or any other Google data. It collects nothing about the channel’s viewers beyond the aggregate numbers YouTube reports to every channel owner.

How the data is used

Only to run the channel: to upload and schedule its Shorts, and to show the owner how they perform so future Shorts can improve. It is not sold, not used for advertising and not used to profile anyone. The app’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Where it is stored

  • The sign-in token is kept as an encrypted environment variable at the app’s host (Vercel) and in a settings file on the owner’s computer.
  • Video titles, upload and publish times, and the statistics above are kept in the app’s database (Neon Postgres) or, when it runs on the owner’s computer, in a local database file.

Sharing

Google user data isn’t shared or sold. The companies that host the app (Vercel) and its database (Neon) store it on the app’s behalf. The characters, the sketches and weekly performance notes are written with Google’s Gemini API, which receives the channel’s settings, trend topics, the sketches and the public view, like and comment counts of the channel’s Shorts, never private analytics or anything else from the owner’s Google account. The characters’ voices, their lip movements and the animation are made on the owner’s computer by open-source software (Kokoro, Rhubarb Lip Sync), so nothing about them is sent anywhere. Background music is downloaded from incompetech.com, which receives no data about the channel or its viewers. The app shows no ads and lets no third party collect information through it.

Keeping and deleting data

Data is kept while the channel uses the app. Access can be revoked at any time on Google’s security settings page, security.google.com/settings/security/permissions. After that the stored token stops working, and the owner can delete the token and the database at any time. Videos already uploaded stay on YouTube until they are removed there.
